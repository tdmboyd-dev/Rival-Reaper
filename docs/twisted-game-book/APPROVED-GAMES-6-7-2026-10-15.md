## Game 6 — SQUARE THE FUCK UP
No Free Spaces. No Easy Wins.

**Equipment:** three giant tic-tac-toe boards; beanbags; ten three-legged straps (two per three-person group); three UNO decks; colored cups; five numbered target cups per active UNO table; bouncing balls; cones; phone timer; referee.

1. Two teams race on a giant tic-tac-toe board. Three runners per team alternate placing beanbags and tagging the next runner. First three in a row wins the board.
2. Winner immediately releases UNO player. Loser must first copy a five-cup color sequence shown for five seconds, then build and unstack a ten-cup pyramid. The five sequence cups must end up at the bottom in correct order.
3. UNO Debt Collector: draw three cards. Number cards = face value; Skip and Reverse = 0; Draw Two = 2; Wild and Draw Four = 4. Add values to determine debt.
4. Bounce small balls once on table into numbered cups 1–5 to subtract points. Reach exactly zero; going over resets debt. Optional gamble: draw one more card to subtract its value; if negative, owe five more.
5. Release three teammates connected by two suitable three-legged straps; they travel around two cones and finish together. First across wins matchup.
6. Five teams play round robin (10 matchups); 3 points per matchup win; rank by wins/points, then award event points 10/8/6/4/2. Universal bottle tiebreaker for ties.
7. No pushing, grabbing or dragging; stop to refit straps.

## Game 7 — THE BAG DON'T LOVE YOU BACK
Everybody Got Baggage. Some of Y'all Got Too Much.

**Equipment:** five of eight sacks; five rubber chickens; five shooting setups (tables or marked surfaces); 25 cups; small bouncing balls; UNO cards; cones; phone timer; referees.

1. All five teams start together. One runner per team retrieves their assigned chicken from the center and brings it to handoff.
2. Sack racer holds chicken under chin against chest, not inside mouth; hops carefully around cones. If dropped, stop, replace, continue.
3. Table player draws one UNO card. 0–4 = one bounce shot in any cup; 5–9 = two bounce shots in two different cups; Skip/Reverse = penalty-cone loop and redraw; Draw Two = three bounce shots; Wild/Draw Four = four bounce shots.
4. Cups at far edge of table; ball must bounce exactly once on table before landing in cup. Retrieve misses and return to starting line.
5. Final runner grabs chicken, squeaks it, crosses finish. Rank finish order 10/8/6/4/2. Universal bottle tiebreaker.
6. Leaving sack early = restart sack section. No unsafe hopping or interference.
