# Game 2 — ONE ROLL, EVERYBODY SUFFER
## Owner-locked dice and pair rules — October 15, 2026

This decision supersedes prior drafts describing dice results as individual cup flips. **A valid roll gives attempts to match entire PAIRS of cups, not single-cup flips.** Do not reintroduce the single-flip interpretation.

### Dice rules (owner-approved)
- Until the team has **two matching pairs**, only dice results **1** or **2** are valid.
- Roll **1**: your cup hunter gets **one full pair attempt** (select two cups); the opposing cup hunter gets **zero** attempts.
- Roll **2**: your cup hunter gets **two full pair attempts**; the opposing cup hunter gets **one full pair attempt**.
- Roll **3, 4, 5 or 6**: **BUSTED**. The roller circles the penalty cone before rolling again.
- **After the team earns two matching pairs**, the roller must roll **exactly 1** to unlock its third and final pair attempt.
- At that stage, **2 is also BUSTED**. Rolls 2–6 are busted, but **the penalty-cone run is waived after the second pair**; the team waits until its next permitted roll to try again.
- A valid pair attempt means the hunter reveals **two cups**. If the hidden items match, the pair is claimed and recorded; otherwise both cups are returned face down. Do not call this a single flip.
- Cap each team at **three claimed pairs**. A third valid pair releases the team stacker. A team cannot take more than three pairs.

### Game equipment and downstream stages retained
- Shared 50-cup matching battlefield, five competing teams. Matching tokens can be pairs of small colored paper slips cut from available paper and marked with colors.
- Stacker uses 15 cups in two or three colors, with their own team's color the majority. Build 5+4+3+2+1 pyramid, collapse to one stack, and put **all rival-colored cups at the bottom** before releasing the thrower.
- Thrower retrieves missed balls, returns behind throwing line, and tries again. Seated catcher must stay seated; three legal catches complete the game. Catcher standing voids that catch and loses one previously completed catch, or 10-second freeze if none. Thrower crossing line voids that throw.
- Proposed overall 12-minute cap, ranking by finish order then stage and progress. Major-game points 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 for 1st through 5th.
- Owner's Shake That Shit bottle tiebreaker concept is documented in `GAME-2-DICE-AND-BOTTLE-TIEBREAKER-2026-10-15.md`.

### Operational note
The one-less-opponent-attempt rule is retained for 1 and 2. Use the prearranged opponent rotation and neutral scorekeeper. Avoid awarding duplicate pairs; remove each claimed pair and log the team. Five teams x three pairs = 15 pairs, so the 50-cup board needs 30 matching-item cups, 10 penalty cups and 10 empty cups as the proposed configuration. Referees must ensure penalty cups are returned or otherwise managed consistently.
