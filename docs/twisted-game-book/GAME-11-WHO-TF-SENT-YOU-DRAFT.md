# GAME 11 — WHO TF SENT YOU?! (DRAFT, NOT APPROVED)

## Identity
Five-team sabotage contest. Four players per team: Boss, Runner, Hustler, Collector. Five tables in a circle. Each team begins with three Hustle Chips (paper slips or bottle caps). First to earn five Exit Stamps and complete a water-cup escape wins.

## Challenge cycles
Every 60 seconds, neutral referee announces one challenge to all teams:
1. JACK YO SPOT — bounce a ball, pick up one jack and catch the ball before the second bounce. Three successful pickups = one stamp.
2. BLOW THE BAG — blow a light ball with a straw across the tabletop into a marked scoring zone three times = one stamp.
3. WET WORK — land two water balloons into a target container from a marked line = one stamp.
A team may earn only one stamp per cycle. Rotate challenges until teams reach five stamps. All teams face the same challenge in each cycle.

## Chaos die
After earning a stamp, the Boss rolls once: 1 TAX THAT ASS — take one unused Hustle Chip from rival; 2 FREEZE THE BLOCK — rival Hustler waits 15 seconds; 3 RUN THEM POCKETS — steal one Exit Stamp from a rival holding two or more; 4 WRONG ADDRESS — two rivals exchange assigned challenge areas next cycle (same challenge difficulty); 5 EVERYBODY EAT — every other team receives one Hustle Chip; 6 WHOLE BLOCK HOT — all teams switch Hustler and Collector for next cycle. Boss can spend one Hustle Chip to cancel a sabotage aimed at own team. Team in last place may spend two chips to force the leader to reroll, replacing result. Referee logs all stamps and chips. No negative chips or stamps.

## Finish and scoring
After five stamps, Boss submits them to referee; Runner carries a marked filled water cup across a short course. If level drops below line, refill and retry. Rank finish order 10/8/6/4/2. Ties use universal SHAKE THAT SHIT.

## Equipment
Five tables, jacks, one light ball per table, drinking straws (one per player), water balloons, five target containers (not yet confirmed owned), five cups, giant die or substitute, marked paper chips and stamps, cones, timer, referees.
