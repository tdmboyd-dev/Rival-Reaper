# Owner-original UNO game — complete recovery; Games 15–16 revisions

## UNO (owner's original invention; no approved title)
13 face-down slots per player in rows 5/5/3: 0–9, Draw Two, Draw Four, Wild. Highest die roll begins; turns counterclockwise. Draw one, place at matching slot, reveal displaced card and chain until card unusable. First to finish gets first place; remaining players **continue until only one unfinished player remains**, giving every player a finishing position. Two players per match preferred, three possible.

Owner-original rules:
- Skip on opponent's unfinished slot blocks placement until Reverse removes Skip. Skip on an activated Draw Two/Draw Four disables its attack, but not completion.
- Activated Draw Two drawn again flips two completed opponent slots back down. Activated Draw Four drawn again flips four completed opponent slots back down.
- Duplicates can stack; three matching cards allow completion of one unfinished slot, with two copies discarded and one retained.
- Triple Wild forces **all opponents** to turn two completed slots face down.
- Two Draw Twos temporarily substitute for a Draw Four, but an actual Draw Four is mandatory for victory; may trade the two Draw Twos for a real Draw Four discarded by another player.
- Proposed, NOT owner-locked: shuffle discard into draw pile when exhausted, one Reverse removes one Skip, attacker chooses which completed opponent slots revert. Note that special-card counts/deck distribution and placement interactions need a written referee resolution, not an invented rule.
- Proposed event placement points 10/8/6/4/2, with team-level scoring method for two-player matches still to be determined. No time limit imposed; suitable as cool-off game.

## Game 15 — WHO STOLE THE KICK?! (revised draft)
Five teams, four roles each: Kicker, Thief, Banker, Collector. Kick at declared close/middle/far zones for 2/4/6 points. One rotating rival can call THAT SHIT MINE before each kick; thief races to retrieve landed ball before kicking team's Collector, stealing half kick value if successful. After two kicks, team can bank its unbanked score or gamble on one extra kick; successful declared-zone kick doubles that kick's value, failure loses half of unbanked points. Once per game team can nominate a rival for SNITCH ROUND: rival's next kick must reach far zone to score. Six regular kicks and one double-value final kick per team, equal chances and turn order. Record banked, unbanked, stolen separately; highest final score ranks. IMPORTANT DRAFT GAPS: clarify theft eligibility on missed called-zone kick, when to bank/zero unbanked, multiple snitch nominations, and precise five-team kick/retrieval rotation. Do not claim owner approved.

## Game 16 — WHO TF CAN YOU TRUST?! (new draft)
Five teams, five players each; hidden roles: 3 Loyal, 1 Snitch, 1 Double Agent. Three rounds of simple prop challenges with chickens/water balloons/cups; captains choose two players to attempt. Snitch can secretly signal to neutral referee, earning a personal betrayal point if team fails. Double Agent may favor a rival, referee records decision. Captain has one accusation per round: correct Snitch accusation +3 team points; wrong -2. Roles revealed after three rounds. Proposed final round player trade across teams, role secrecy retained. IMPORTANT: team scoring and saboteur incentives require balancing before owner approval; cannot leave scoring undefined in final rules. Do not require dangerous physical interference.
