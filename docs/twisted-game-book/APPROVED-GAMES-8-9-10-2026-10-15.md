## Game 8: PULL UP OR SHUT UP
Two teams of five pullers; best two of three. Losing team rolls chaos die after each pull. Before first pull, high captain roll chooses Steal a Body or Switch the Script.

**1 — MANDATORY STEAL A BODY:** captain selects an eligible, pre-consenting rope-game participant from a team not currently pulling. Selection is mandatory as a game mechanic, replacing one of the five pullers; no tactical refusal, but a person may opt out for personal safety or physical limitations. Choose another eligible participant if unavailable. **2 — SWITCH THE SCRIPT:** select rope side and order. **3 — BALLOON WARRANT:** opponents must catch 2 of 4 balloons in a separate area before next pull. **4 — ONE-LEG TAX:** opposing middle puller does ten hops before next pull. **5 — BENCH EVERYBODY:** both teams swap two pullers. **6 — DOUBLE OR NOTHING:** next pull earns a bonus matchup point for its winner.

No one-leg rope pulls or throwing water balloons at people actively holding a tensioned rope; these twists happen between pulls. Ten round-robin matchups, 3 points per matchup victory plus bonus, final placement 10/8/6/4/2, universal Shake That Shit tiebreaker.

## Game 9: SIT YO ASS DOWN
Ten players (two from each of five teams), ten chairs, four hidden rubber-chicken markers. Walk while music plays; on stop, claim one chair by touching its back, do not sit. Ref reveals chickens; marked chair means eliminated. Reset markers secretly between rounds. Last survivor wins. Run equal heats; 1 survival point per advanced round, +2 last survivor; rank teams, award 10/8/6/4/2; universal tiebreaker. No blindfolded running or chair shoving.

## Game 10: THE GREAT WATER BALLOON ROBBERY
Five teams at once; Runner, Robber, Banker, Guard. Ten balloons per team. Runner brings own balloon to banker; Robber takes from rivals' marked exposed pickup zones; Guard decides how many to expose or protect, without grabbing/blocking. Banker rolls die: 1 bank +1; 2 bank +2; 3 give a balloon or lose 2; 4 land balloon in cup for +4; 5 trade two banked balloons for +5; 6 steal and deliver rival pickup balloon for +6. Six-minute round, +1 per delivered balloon in final minute, one accidental-burst insurance claim per team. Track scored balloons so none count twice. Rank 10/8/6/4/2; universal tiebreaker.
