# UNIVERSAL RIVAL DAY III TIEBREAKER — SHAKE THAT SHIT

**Applies to EVERY game**, including individual games, overall placement and the championship. This replaces conflicting per-game sudden-death instructions. Event host approval of exact setup is still needed; this records the owner's direction to use a single consistent system.

## The setup
- Prepare up to ~100 identical half-full clear water bottles with a concealed, securely closed, food-safe coloring mechanism in the cap. Five possible reveal colors: red, pink, orange, blue, green.
- Use equal quantities of all five colors, mixed randomly; prepare a separate supply of unused bottles for later ties.
- No one may see or feel which color is hidden. Do not drink colored game bottles.
- Have a neutral scorekeeper, die and an event-wide score sheet.

## One tie, one procedure
1. Only teams tied for the relevant place participate. Their representatives roll one die each; highest goes first; ties reroll. Continue in descending order.
2. In that order, each tied team picks **one unopened bottle** and shakes it.
3. If the bottle reveals **that team's own color**, that team immediately wins the tie.
4. If it reveals another team's color, the team of that color receives **two overall-event bonus points**, even if that team is not in this tiebreaker. The tied team's turn ends; the next tied team picks.
5. Continue in the same order until a tied team reveals its own color. Used bottles leave play.
6. The two-point bonuses do not add another team to the current tie. They do affect subsequent overall-event standings.
7. If the bottle supply runs out without a winner, replenish with a fresh, equally balanced hidden-color set and continue. Do not improvise a different game.

## Referee calls
- A bottle is invalid if opened, visibly marked, leaking, tampered with or not revealing clearly. Replace it with an unused bottle; no points awarded for that invalid bottle.
- Once a tie is resolved, record the winner and any bonus points on the master sheet before starting another tie.
- If bonuses create a new overall-score tie, use this same procedure again.
- Food coloring inside bottle caps may leak or fail to transfer. Prepare a small sample beforehand to check the mechanism; if it fails, use concealed colored slips inside opaque containers with the **same reveal-and-award rules**, rather than switching the scoring game.
- Keep these bottles away from drinking-water stations.

## How this works with game scoring
The standard major-game placements remain 10 / 8 / 6 / 4 / 2 points for 1st–5th, and quick-game placements are proposed at 5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1. Resolve ties in placements with this procedure before awarding placement points. Two-point bottle bonuses are **additional overall-event points**, not game-placement points.

**Timing caveat:** The event is tomorrow. Preparing 100 concealed-cap bottles may take significant time. A smaller equal-color batch can run the same game, provided the colors are equally represented and the supply can be replenished.
