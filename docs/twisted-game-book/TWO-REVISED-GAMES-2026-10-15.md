# Two revised games — owner review draft

## Protect Yo Problem — Everybody Want Smoke
Five teams play simultaneously. Each team has one Problem, three protectors, a shooter, and a table defender. Five tables, each defended by a different team color. A visiting Problem challenges the defender at a rival table; neutral referee judges. If Problem wins, referee awards a colored-ball key. If defender wins, the defender's team gets that table's key; the visitor must go around a return cone and try another table. Teams need three distinct table keys; duplicates do not count. A defender may show an earned key to their own returning Problem. Tables remain open after a win. Each table's safe zone prohibits dodgeball throws. Dodgeballs only in marked field lanes, soft balls, below shoulder height, no grabbing or tackling.

Five proposed table challenges:
1. Who The Fuck Lying: three hidden UNO number cards and true/false clues, choose highest.
2. Don't Drop The Bag: ten cups per contestant with balls on top; first six flip-and-catches wins, 45-second cap.
3. Copy That Shit: referee displays a multi-color eight-cup arrangement for five seconds; recreate exact positions/colors in 30 seconds.
4. Roll Or Get Rolled: die determines equal ring-toss attempts; most successful wins.
5. Square The Fuck Up: small tic-tac-toe; first three in a row, draw resets.

After three distinct keys, Problem runs the marked finish lane; hits require retry of escape, not loss of earned keys. Suggested 12-minute cap. Exact staffing, tie-break and safety setup require field rehearsal and owner approval.

## The Domino Effect — One Roll, Everybody Suffer
Five teams at once, five active players each: dice roller, cup hunter, cup stacker, thrower, seated catcher. Each team gets ten upside-down cups (50 total), concealing five matching pairs of colored tokens/balls. Die rolls 1–3 grant that many cup flips; 4–6 means roller runs penalty cone before next roll. Players reveal cups, claim matched pairs, return unmatched cups face down. Odd extra flip remains visible until next roll, but cannot be claimed alone. After all five pairs, stacker builds 10-cup pyramid (4+3+2+1) and collapses it to one neat stack; then thrower must complete three seated cup catches from marked distance. First completed wins; proposed seven-minute cap. Requires verification of actual matching-color tokens and equal table/field lanes. Owner approval pending.
