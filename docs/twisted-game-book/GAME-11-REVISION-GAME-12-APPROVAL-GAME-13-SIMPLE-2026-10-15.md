## Game 11 revised owner mechanics
Five teams send competitors to mixed-team tables. At each table: 5 competitors, 3 available ring Hustle Chips; each chip worth 10 points; one Exit award worth another 10 points. Players who win no chips earn 0 table points. The scoring mechanism must be reconciled before approval: owner suggests minimum 2/3 successful challenges to qualify, yet also wants one player to accumulate most of 3 chips to win Exit. Proposed solution (NOT owner locked): each of three rounds awards its one ring to the highest-performing qualified competitor under an explicit timed/precision rule, with a fair tie-break; alternatively one chip per qualified player plus Exit decided separately. Do not assert both incompatible schemes as final.

Table 1 JACK YO SPOT: five players roll die for order (highest first, ties reroll). Three successive tasks, one attempt per player per task: bounce small ball, pick up exactly 1, then 3, then 5 jacks and catch ball; no touching extra jacks. If two competitors complete all three, tie must be resolved at end, not prematurely. At least 2/3 successful attempts to qualify for Hustle Chip under owner's tentative rule.

Table 2 BLOW THE BAG: five rivals at one table. Referee tapes cup at CENTER round 1, RIGHT round 2, FAR END round 3; all contestants blow lightweight ball with straw from opposite side; one attempt per round. Same 2/3 qualification proposal.

Tables 3, 4, 5 remain draft: Wet Work water balloon target; Ring That Ass ring toss; Don't Touch My Shit hidden-cup memory. Their scoring must be made consistent with the chip allocation once owner confirms.

Physical equipment: ring toss rings proposed as Hustle Chips, count not verified; 3 rings per table = 15 total needed if all five tables run simultaneously. ## Game 13 simplified player-facing draft
**DON'T LET THE BLOCK FIND OUT — THE WHOLE SQUAD GOT BAD INFORMATION**
Four players per team. Ref secretly tells Player 1 three tasks in order. Player 1 gets ten seconds to memorize. They tell Player 2, who tells Player 3, who tells Player 4. Player 4 completes tasks in order: swinging-ball challenge, squeak chicken five times, bounce three white balls into cups. First team to finish correctly wins. Before starting, die determines how many words Player 1 may use: 1–2 = five words, 3–4 = ten, 5–6 = fifteen. Each team gets one 'THAT AIN'T WHAT I SAID' correction, costing 20 seconds. No written instruction passed between players. Equipment: swinging-ball kits (4 ordered, arrival unverified), chicken, white balls, cups, die, referee task cards; for five teams run equal heats if only four apparatus available. Standard placement points 10/8/6/4/2; universal SHAKE THAT SHIT tiebreaker.
