## Game 8 — Pull Up or Shut Up (revised draft)
Two teams, five pullers per team, best two of three pulls. After each pull the losing captain rolls the chaos die:
1. Steal a Body — replace one of your five with a willing noncompeting teammate.
2. Switch the Script — choose rope side and pulling lineup.
3. Water Balloon Warrant — opponent's two players catch two of four water balloons in a separate splash area before the next pull.
4. One-Leg Tax — opponent's middle puller does ten single-leg hops in a separate marked area before next pull.
5. Bench Everybody — both teams replace two pullers with rested teammates.
6. Double or Nothing — losing team wagers a bonus matchup point on the next pull; if they lose the opposing team gets the bonus.
No water balloons thrown at actively pulling participants and no one-leg balancing under rope tension: perform dice-triggered tasks between pulls. Owner requested more aggressive twists; these are the workable variants. Ten round-robin matchups, event placement 10/8/6/4/2, universal Shake That Shit tiebreaker.

## Game 9 — Sit Yo Ass Down (draft)
Ten chairs, five teams x two players; hidden rubber-chicken markers under four chairs. Music plays; players walk, then claim chairs by touching backs when it stops. Referee reveals hidden chickens; marked players eliminated. Reset markers and continue until one remains. No blindfolded running; optional blind selection while stationary. Equal team participation across heats. The owner is reviewing the previously proposed UNO twist; avoid making cards mandatory.

## Game 10 — Great Water Balloon Robbery (draft)
Five teams simultaneously, four roles: Runner, Robber, Banker, Guard. Ten balloons per team, separate unguarded rival pickup zones, no snatching from hands. Runner delivers own balloon; Robber may retrieve rival pickup balloon. Banker rolls: 1 keep +1, 2 keep +2, 3 give balloon or lose 2, 4 land balloon in cup +4, 5 trade two balloons +5, 6 collect a rival pickup balloon +6 after delivery. Six-minute round, last minute each delivered balloon +1, one insurance claim per team, highest score wins, event placement 10/8/6/4/2 and universal tiebreaker. The Guard role needs final practical definition before approval.
