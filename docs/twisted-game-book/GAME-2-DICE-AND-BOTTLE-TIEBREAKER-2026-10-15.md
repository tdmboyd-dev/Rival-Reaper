# Game 2 latest owner corrections — October 15, 2026

**Status: draft pending final owner approval.** This is the newest owner direction and supersedes the earlier dice rule in TWO-REVISED-GAMES-2026-10-15.md.

## Dice / cup hunt
- One shared table, 50 face-down cups. Use marked colored paper slips instead of special tokens if necessary.
- Five teams participate; each team can claim at most three matching pairs.
- **Before two pairs:** only rolls of **1 or 2** award flips. Roll 1: active hunter gets 1 flip, next opposing hunter gets 0; roll 2: active hunter gets 2 flips, opposing hunter gets 1. Rolls 3–6 require roller to circle penalty cone before retrying.
- **After two pairs:** roller must roll exactly **1** to unlock the third-match attempt. No penalty-cone running from that point; other results do not unlock the attempt.
- Ambiguity to resolve before printing: a single flip cannot ordinarily complete a pair unless a prior revealed cup remains available. Ensure a fair, unambiguous pairing mechanism and that all five teams can obtain three pairs from the shared pool. Do not pretend this is already verified.

## Final stages
- Stacker builds 15-cup pyramid (5+4+3+2+1) using 2–3 cup colors, majority in own team color. Unstack to one pile with **all rival-colored cups at the bottom** before releasing thrower.
- Thrower stands behind marked line, retrieves misses and returns behind line; seated catcher holds cup and must remain seated. Three valid catches to finish. Standing invalidates catch and causes loss of one previously earned catch (or 10-second freeze at zero). Step over throw line invalidates throw.
- Proposed game cap: 12 minutes. First to finish ranks first; if timed out, rank by stage completed, then pairs, then valid catches. Event major-game points proposed 10/8/6/4/2.

## Proposed Rival Day tiebreaker: SHAKE THAT SHIT — FATE IN A BOTTLE
- About 100 half-filled identical water bottles, food coloring hidden inside caps; bottle shaken to reveal one of five team colors.
- Tied teams roll for pick order, highest first, ties reroll.
- A tied team revealing its own color wins the tiebreaker.
- A noncompeting team whose color is revealed receives **two overall-score bonus points**; those bonus points do not change eligibility for the current tiebreak.
- Continue picks until a tied team reveals its own color.
- Requires a reliable, non-leaking and genuinely concealed cap-color mechanism; test before event, since ordinary food coloring may leak or become visible. Food coloring stains.
- Owner approval pending; do not call final until exact matching-turn mechanic is resolved.
